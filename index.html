About Us

AISA is a globally recognized private school in Abu Dhabi, UAE. We offer both the International Baccalaureate (IB) and US education systems.

Our Curriculum

AISA provides a comprehensive curriculum that prepares students for success in international education. Our program boasts top-notch IB and US courses.

Programs Offered

  • AISA International Program: Offers IB curriculum with a strong emphasis on global citizenship.
  • AISA American Program: Provides a US-based education with excellent resources and facilities.
  • AISA Global Program: Combines IB and US elements, offering a unique educational model.
