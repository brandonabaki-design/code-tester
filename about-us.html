About Us

AISA is a globally recognized private school in Abu Dhabi, UAE. We offer both the International Baccalaureate (IB) and US education systems.

AISA History

AISA was founded in 2005 with the goal of providing high-quality education to students from around the world. Since then, our school has grown exponentially and is now one of the leading international schools in Abu Dhabi.

AISA Mission

The mission of AISA is to provide students with a holistic education that fosters critical thinking, creativity, and global citizenship. We strive to create a safe, inclusive, and supportive learning environment where students can reach their full potential.

AISA Facilities

AISA has state-of-the-art facilities that include modern classrooms, sports fields, and a library. We also offer a variety of co-curricular activities to support students' development both inside and outside the classroom.

AISA Achievements

AISA has been recognized for its excellence in education and is frequently highlighted by global educational organizations. Our students consistently achieve top grades on IB exams and have gone on to pursue higher education at some of the world's leading institutions.
